# Provenance: The shape of an answer

Why [the rule](../../claude/rules/answer-shape.md) exists.

All three come from one session: a question about Android share-sheet ordering
whose real answer was "long-press → Pin; the contact
row cannot be turned off". It took a two-column table, four numbered remedies, two
of them retracted the following turn, three rounds of pushback, and finally a
"SIMPLER" that deleted the only forward-looking idea in the message.

The fourth, *verify before excluding*, came on 2026-11-12 while pricing earbuds across
Turkish shops. A music shop was written off as selling no earbuds after one search for
AirPods and a read of a page that had not finished loading. Its menu had a consumer
headphone category, and it stocked two of the Marshall models being priced. A
pattern-discovery run found no earlier instance in the logs from 2026-09-11 on, and no
rule covered a claim that a source lacks something. The fix is that paragraph, plus a
line in the search-turkish-shops skill.
